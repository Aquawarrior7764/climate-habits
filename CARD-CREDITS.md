## Files studied and adapted

Upstream commit: `acb1197633e749a1fba4412231db2f6581586d00`.

- [`src/lib/components/Card.svelte`](https://github.com/simeydotme/pokemon-cards-css/blob/acb1197633e749a1fba4412231db2f6581586d00/src/lib/components/Card.svelte): cursor percentages; rotation of `-(x - 50) / 3.5` and `(y - 50) / 3.5`; background ranges 37–63% / 33–67%; glare position, opacity, distance from center; interaction springs `.066 / .25`; 500ms exit delay followed by `.01 / .06` soft return. The popover adapts `setCenter()`, `popover()` and `retreat()`: translation, scale and spin springs `.033 / .45`, with the same 1.75x enlargement cap. A full turn plus a half turn (540°) reveals the educational back; the original uses 360° and finishes on the front. Closing reverses the spin into the original grid slot.
- [`src/lib/helpers/Math.js`](https://github.com/simeydotme/pokemon-cards-css/blob/acb1197633e749a1fba4412231db2f6581586d00/src/lib/helpers/Math.js): clamp and linear range mapping, inlined in the adaptation.
- [`public/css/cards/base.css`](https://github.com/simeydotme/pokemon-cards-css/blob/acb1197633e749a1fba4412231db2f6581586d00/public/css/cards/base.css): 600px perspective, rotation order, trading-card aspect/radius, front/back transforms, shadows, pointer-position radial glare, blend modes.
- [`public/css/cards/regular-holo.css`](https://github.com/simeydotme/pokemon-cards-css/blob/acb1197633e749a1fba4412231db2f6581586d00/public/css/cards/regular-holo.css): moving rainbow/scanline composite, background multipliers, luminosity mask; reduced opacity and saturation, confined to the artwork area. Studied `cards.css`, `basic.css`, and `reverse-holo.css` for shared variables and alternatives.

The numeric spring integrator is adapted from [Svelte 3.52.0 `src/runtime/motion/spring.ts`](https://github.com/sveltejs/svelte/blob/v3.52.0/src/runtime/motion/spring.ts), copyright (c) 2016–22 the Svelte contributors, under MIT. Its complete notice is retained in [licenses/svelte-MIT.txt](licenses/svelte-MIT.txt). No Svelte runtime or framework dependency is shipped. Fixed 60Hz spring steps accumulated inside `requestAnimationFrame` keep the physics consistent on high-refresh displays and bound long-frame catch-up; only unsettled cards run.

No Pokémon images, logos, card backs, third-party foil textures, or remote image dependencies are included. `images/card-art-placeholder.svg` is a simple local placeholder created for this integration. Fronts contain only an image and border. Back titles and single short paragraphs are samples for replacement, not finished educational content.

## Scope and intentional differences

The supplied repository had one branch (`main`) and one commit (`80af215`); both game sections were placeholders. The user selected Food for this foundation. There was no game score or progression system to migrate. Other pages, the checklist, and the editor are preserved. Cards emit a bubbling `climatecardflip` event with `detail.flipped` for future game integration; flipping itself does not award points or persist a score.

The motion reproduces the original pointer mapping, springs, perspective, layered lighting and centered popover. Differences: a stable hit area to avoid cursor feedback; a 540° spin to reveal the back on every opening; a native modal dialog with focus containment and outside-click/Escape/close-button dismissal; no gyroscope or automatic showcase; lighter, texture-free foil; zero animation under reduced motion. Touch keeps normal page scrolling in the deck and tap-to-open; the background is locked while a card is open. All faces are available without JavaScript and in print. Edit mode exposes front and back together and pauses card motion.

Keep back copy concise enough for a trading card. Inspect longer titles and text on mobile after editing. The component is reusable: copy an entire `article[data-climate-card]`, use unique face IDs, and update its button's `aria-controls`. The script reads the title from the back at initialization and after editing, so label changes stay accessible without front text.
