A 2017 study compared dozens of things individuals can do about climate change. Diet and car use both landed in its short list of “high-impact” actions, far ahead of the tips people hear most often (Wynes and Nicholas).

0.8 tons of greenhouse gases saved per person every year by eating a plant-based diet

2.4 tons of greenhouse gases by living car-free

4× MORE than recycling saves (compared with a plant-based diet)

Figures are averages for developed countries from Wynes and Nicholas (2017).

Those numbers describe going all the way. Most of us won't, at least not at first, and that is fine. The rest of this page is about the steps in between.

Transportation habits

Why it matters

Living without a car saves about 2.4 tons of greenhouse gases per person every year. Wynes and Nicholas say that it's one of the four most effective ways to cut greenhouse gases for a person.

Going fully car-free is not realistic for everyone, but partial changes help too.

26–76%

lower emissions when a trip moves from a sedan to public transit, even over the same distance

Research by Chester et al. (2013), as reported in Wynes and Nicholas (2017).

CHANGES YOU CAN MAKE

  • Public Transport
  • Carpooling
  • Walking/Biking
  • Do multiple errands at once
  • Fly on planes less
